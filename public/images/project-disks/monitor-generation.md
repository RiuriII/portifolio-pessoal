# Monitor frame

Generated with built-in image_gen, transparent_background=true. Center and exterior alpha checked.

Use case: product-mockup. Generate ONE vintage beige CRT desktop monitor FRAME for use as a website dialog overlay. Same tactile realism as a 1990s beige floppy disk: warm off-white aged plastic with subtle grain, gently worn edges, tiny charcoal details. Strict straight-on front orthographic view, symmetrical, no perspective or tilt. Landscape canvas 1536x1024. Monitor occupies nearly all canvas with only small 3% margins, no bulky stand, no keyboard, no desk, no background or shadow outside. Thick rounded beige rectangular bezel, large rounded rectangular hollow screen opening occupying approximately x=12%..88%, y=12%..78% of the entire canvas. CRITICAL the screen opening must be genuinely transparent alpha like a hole, NOT black, NOT white, NO glass, NO reflection, NO checkerboard. Exterior around monitor also genuine transparent alpha. Only the physical bezel is opaque. Bottom bezel contains discreet horizontal speaker slots at left and small green status LED at right. Do not draw a button with text; actual functional controls will be added as HTML. A small pale yellow paper post-it stuck on the top-right bezel, NOT covering the screen opening, with exact handwritten text "Riuri Dev". Premium art direction, restrained realistic material, clean silhouette. Frame only, empty transparent screen, no content.
